## Evidence rules

- Check results belong to the exact source, build, profile, inputs and environment
  that produced them. Authored tests and campaigns are not recorded executions.
- Canonical denial or uncertainty cannot be overridden by logs, JUnit, callbacks,
  exit status, source availability or a status badge.
- Hardware, two-host, machine-loss, signed-output and external-provider blockers
  stay visible. Complete reachable implementation/checks without fabricating
  their missing observations or weakening acceptance criteria.
