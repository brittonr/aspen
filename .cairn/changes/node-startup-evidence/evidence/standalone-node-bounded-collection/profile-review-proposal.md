## Candidate contract for owner decision

1. An owner-versioned **new** profile would derive its lint inventory from the canonical catalog, prove every named lint registered, and fail on an unclassified added/removed lint. Keep `-D unknown_lints`; require all 72 currently catalogued lints active on every selected compilation unit. Do not use `disabled_lints`, a warning count ceiling, a silent `allow` override, or a consumer-controlled environment knob as admission. A proposed minimal delta from the reviewed owner's all-deny hook is exactly the seven named advisory categories above; retain deny for the other 65, including `usize_in_public_api`, `no_unwrap`, `no_panic`, bounded-resource checks, unchecked conversion/arithmetic, suppression governance, marked state machines, and exact nominal-domain checks. The owner must review the consequences of enabling nominal-domain mode and scope-specific rules; the older installed hook and a bare `bounded-systems` run do not prove their coverage. **Bind the exact effective lint-level argv or an owner-generated equivalent to the profile identity**: equal hashes for the two hooks above prove that the current workspace-metadata fields alone are insufficient.
2. Advisory does **not** mean ignored. Retain every emitted finding and require a finite, expiring, exact source/profile/config-bound review decision for each observed instance. Reject a new, changed, unkeyed, expired, or unreviewed finding; reject any hard finding. The existing Molten `quarantine-ci` warning baseline has keyed findings, expiry and no-new-finding checks, but **is not normal-node startup authority** and its count/shrink-target mechanism is not a substitute for the proposed exact review. The owner must choose and verify the review format and approval authority before any policy becomes operative.
3. Preserve `[workspace.metadata.octet]` selection of `molten-node-runtime`, `molten-node-core`, and `molten-node-host` with `--all-targets`. Reconcile actual compiler units, build scripts, generated sources, proc macros/local dependencies, and source-to-object inputs with the complete executable closure. Record owner revision, all profile/catalog and config hashes, exact Rust/Dylint/Octet binary identities, compiler/build inputs, and executable digest. A source list asserted by the claimant or a clean one-crate count cannot prove compilation or exhaustive membership.
4. Molten's `src/octet/startup_snapshot.rs` currently accepts only `DEFAULT_GATE_COMMAND` and zero total/warnings/errors, and the gate names `strict-ci`; `.cairn/changes/node-startup-evidence/specs/node-startup-evidence/spec.md` explicitly rejects warning budgets. The producer must **first** accept a reviewed spec/policy change for exact per-finding decisions, then change its verifier and migrate every normal-node caller in one cutover. Keep the old strict gate denied throughout proposal review. Verify negative cases: stale/mismatched profile/catalog/config/**effective lint flags**; unknown or missing lint; disabled lint; new hard or unreviewed advisory; omitted or reordered compiled source; fabricated tool status; stale build inputs; mismatched executable; source mutation after review. A synthetic clean receipt or a passing `quarantine-ci` baseline must still fail normal startup.
5. After an owner-approved profile and producer acceptance exist, rerun from a compatible existing compiler/toolchain without Stage0. Require complete clean hard-lint results and accepted exact advisory decisions across the **entire** selected closure, independent compiler/source/build/binary binding, real node startup, and then the separate consumer VM and replay requirements. An Octet profile run alone has no authority to set `execution_established` or `startup_authorized`.

Owner review must decide whether these seven categories may be advisory under a versioned startup policy, whether each of the 255 current findings is reviewed or instead corrected, and whether the proposed contract is adequate. Producer spec review must independently decide whether zero-warning startup can be replaced by exact reviewed findings. **Neither decision has been made.** If either is rejected, preserve the unchanged strict denial and pursue semantics-preserving source fixes instead.
