## Why

Audit of Aspen's scheduling/lease (`fabric_time`), membership/placement (`fabric_membership`), and coordination (`coordination`) state machines uncovered permanent state wedges, resource leaks, and fencing-token defects:

1. **Time reservation `Stale` trap and permanent worker capacity leak (`crates/molten-core/src/fabric_time/reservation/batch.rs:142-165`, `reservation/mod.rs:224-225`):** When a reservation expires with `enforcement: None`, `expire_reservation` moves its state from `Committed` to `Stale`. When an `ObservedStop` or `FencingTakeover` fact arrives later, `expire_reservation` checks `state != Committed` and returns `Err(ExpiryIssue::NotStale)` with no other release path—leaking the capacity forever. Even on immediate `Release`, `expire_reservation` restores `next.pool` but **never restores `next.workers[worker_id].{units,bytes}`**, permanently depleting workers until all future reservations fail with `NoWorkerAvailable`.
2. **Partial batch allocation rollback and unfenced lease renewal (`src/fabric_time/shell/reservation.rs:90-119`, `crates/molten-core/src/fabric_time/lease.rs:231-248`):** `Owner::reserve_batch` updates `self.state` only after every item finishes `self.effects.allocate`; if item 2 fails after item 1 succeeded externally, `self.state` reverts to pre-batch capacity (allowing double-allocation) and flattens `FabricPortError::Uncertain` into `Failure::invalid_harness`. Meanwhile `evaluate_lease` grants `LeaseAction::Renew` whenever `Pending` without checking `request.consistency`, `fencing_token`, or `previous_fencing_token`, letting stale or unfenced holders renew exclusive leases.
3. **Permanent role-slot bricking in membership persistence and quarantine (`src/fabric_membership/persistence.rs:69-182`, `persistence/codec.rs:23-95`, `crates/molten-core/src/fabric_membership/transition.rs:62-64, 348-350, 435-468`):**
   - If `run_role_effect` fails (even with `effect_may_have_happened: false`) or crashes after `record_intent` writes `pending = Some(...)` in Redb, `current()`, `record_intent()`, and successor `initialize()` all fail closed permanently because `AssignmentPersistence` has no inspect-pending, abort-pre-effect, or reconcile transition.
   - `AssignmentState::Quarantined` is terminal with no transition to `Released`, yet `validate_assignment_successor` requires `predecessor.state == Released`—so quarantining an assignment permanently bricks that `(extension_id, service_id, role_id)` slot.
4. **Placement and failure-observation defects (`crates/molten-core/src/fabric_membership/mod.rs:622-651, 963-1005`):** When `allow_degraded == true`, `PlacementSearch::select` does not rank healthy nodes ahead of `Suspected`/`Unavailable` nodes, so a higher-preference `Unavailable` node is picked ahead of healthy nodes. Failure observation reduction is input-order dependent on equal `(observed_at_ticks, class)` and compares raw tick integers across distinct `time_basis_ref`s.
5. **Coordination placeholder fencing tokens, broken assertion refs, and unexpiring locks/elections (`src/coordination/parts/mod/p002..p005`):** Committed `LockState`/`ElectionState` retain `placeholder.token_ref` instead of the issued `commit_receipt_ref`-bound `FencingToken.token_ref`; `receipt.dataspace_assertion_refs` binds a discarded placeholder hash instead of the emitted `CoordinationStatusAssertion`; locks and elections have no lease expiry or resignation transition (a crashed holder permanently wedges the key); and `new_coordination_runtime` silently hardcodes the single-process fixture manifest ignoring the caller's `control_group_ref`.

## What Changes

- Fix `expire_reservation` to accept `Committed` or `Stale` reservations when enforcement arrives and to restore both `next.pool` and `next.workers[worker_id]` capacity on `Release`.
- Make `Owner::reserve_batch` commit state incrementally per externally allocated item and preserve `FabricPortError::Uncertain`; require `LeaseAction::Renew` to validate fencing token monotonicity and consistency rules.
- Add explicit pending-intent inspection/pre-effect-abort/reconciliation methods to `AssignmentPersistence` and allow authorized successor initialization after `Quarantined` (with explicit reconciliation/fencing evidence); rank healthy placement candidates strictly ahead of degraded ones; make failure-observation reduction order-independent and scoped per `time_basis_ref`.
- Fix coordination to persist real `FencingToken.token_ref` values in committed state, bind `receipt.dataspace_assertion_refs` to the actual emitted `CoordinationStatusAssertion`, add explicit lock lease expiry and election resignation/step-down transitions, and stop silently substituting a hardcoded fixture manifest for `control_group_ref`.

## Impact

- **Files**: `crates/molten-core/src/fabric_time/{reservation/batch.rs,lease.rs}`, `src/fabric_time/shell/reservation.rs`, `crates/molten-core/src/fabric_membership/{transition.rs,mod.rs}`, `src/fabric_membership/{ports.rs,shell.rs,persistence.rs,persistence/codec.rs}`, and `src/coordination/parts/mod/{p002,p003,p004,p005}/body.rs`.
- **Verification**: Unit and property tests covering delayed `Stale -> Released` enforcement, worker-capacity conservation across allocate/release cycles, partial batch allocation failure, stale-token `Renew` denial, pending-intent and `Quarantined` role-slot recovery, degraded-vs-healthy placement ordering, cross-time-basis failure observation isolation, coordination `token_ref`/assertion ref parity, and lock expiry / election resignation.
- **Non-claims**: Fixing local and pure state-machine wedges makes recovery and fencing sound within Aspen's control models; it does not confer distributed linearizability onto the single-process control registry.

## Out of Scope

- Replacing the single-process `raft_control_plane` with replicated `fabric_consistency::raft` across all coordination services (separate architecture decision).
- Replicated Raft protocol bugs (tracked in `repair-live-raft-liveness-safety-and-transport-auth`).
