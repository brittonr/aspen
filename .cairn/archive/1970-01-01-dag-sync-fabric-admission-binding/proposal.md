## Why

The archived DAG synchronization package provides a real decision core and ordered shell, but no fabric descriptor or canonical binding that another system extension can require. Federation must not turn a conformance adapter's fixed references into production admission.

## What Changes

- Register the existing DAG shell through its owning `dag_sync` module with canonical request/receipt schema identities, exact operation, declared authority/resources, and caller-supplied reviewed profile and conformance references.
- Require the federation manifest to bind that exact admitted descriptor and route the supervised callback effect through `run_dag_sync`.
- Preserve receiver selection, content verification, current authority/resource admission and durable progress checks. Neither descriptor possession nor graph completion grants import or merge authority.

## Impact

Domain-local DAG descriptor exports and federation composition change. No new dependency, backend, transport identity, default extension activation, or global admission authority is introduced. Runtime checks, review, validation and archive remain required; proposal status is not admission.
