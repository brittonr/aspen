## Why

Audit of Aspen's world lifecycle subsystems (`src/world_promotion/`, `src/world_head/`, `crates/molten-core/src/world_merge/`, and `crates/molten-core/src/worldcommit/`) identified severe authority-confusion, crash-wedge, receipt-falsification, and admission-bypass bugs:

1. **World Promotion cross-action signature confusion, stale grants, and delimiter-injectable Ed25519 messages (`src/world_promotion/current.rs:34-57, 144-228`, `operator/mod.rs:21-102`, `crates/molten-core/src/world_promotion/operator.rs:35`, `planning/mod.rs:84`):** `SignedPromotionAuthority` binds only `(branch_id, authority_ref, policy_ref, generation, admitted)`—omitting action/purpose, `candidate_head`, `reservation_ref`, nonce/consumption, and expiry. A single grant at generation `G` intended to `deny` or `abandon` a reservation from `G-1 -> G` is cryptographically identical to a grant authorizing `promote` of *any* candidate head from `G -> G+1`. Operator mutations (`deny_reservation`, `plan_retry_for_reservation`, `abandon_uncertain_for_attempt`) never check the current head from `WORLD_HEADS_TABLE`. Signing messages serialize fields with raw `\n` and `,` delimiters without rejecting them in inputs rather than using canonical packed Preserves.
2. **World Promotion multi-transaction dispatch crash wedges, TOCTOU, and broken read-back (`src/world_promotion/service.rs:164-237`, `store/outbox.rs:27-113`, `store/transaction.rs:23-27, 120-139`, `operator/mod.rs:84-101`):** `dispatch_world_reservation` writes `update_reservation(Attempting)` and `store_attempt(Attempting)` (and later terminal updates) in separate Redb write transactions; crashing between them permanently wedges the reservation in `Attempting` with no recovery path. `abandon_uncertain_for_attempt` never transitions the reservation out of `Uncertain`. `read_back_promotion` classifies partial persistence (`head == plan.after` with missing reservations) as `Prior` instead of `Corrupt` and breaks idempotent replay once a reservation leaves `Committed`. `update_reservation` fails to verify immutability of reservation identity fields, and `validate_promotion_transaction` runs outside `begin_write()`.
3. **World Head false `"admitted"` receipts and unsigned carrier trust (`src/world_head/parts/service/p000/body.rs:194-238`, `p001/body.rs:2-19`):** `execute_world_head_transition` constructs `admitted_receipt` before calling `store.apply_transition` and returns that `"admitted"` receipt even when CAS returns `Stale` or `Uncertain` (also persisting `"admitted"` into `WORLD_HEAD_UNCERTAIN_TABLE`). Signer admission trusts caller-supplied unsigned `carrier.role` and `carrier.authority_admitted` rather than verifying them against signed statements and policy.
4. **World Merge & World Commit admission bypasses (`crates/molten-core/src/world_merge/admission.rs:105-126`, `planning/application.rs:9-70`, `diff.rs:44-54`, `worldcommit/restore.rs:198-225`):** `reduce_root` short-circuits equal `left`/`right` roots to `Selected` *before* checking `is_runtime_sensitive`, `available`, and `profile.root_modes`; `WorldApplicationHandlerProfile.input_schema` is never enforced against `base`/`left`/`right` schemas; `diff_world_roots` misclassifies branch creation/deletion as `Absent`; and `worldcommit::validate_parent_graph` ignores unreachable nodes and detached cycles (`X -> Y -> X`).

## What Changes

- Bind `SignedPromotionAuthority` to explicit action/purpose, exact target (`candidate_head` or `reservation_ref`), nonce/consumption, and expiry over canonical packed Preserves bytes; enforce current branch head checks on operator mutations.
- Combine reservation and attempt state transitions into single atomic Redb write transactions, transition reservations on abandonment, verify immutable fields in `update_reservation`, fix `read_back_promotion` partial-commit classification and idempotent replay, and validate transaction facts inside `begin_write()`.
- Ensure `execute_world_head_transition` emits distinct canonical receipts for `Applied`, `Stale`, and `Uncertain` outcomes and derives `role` and `authority_admitted` strictly from verified signed statements and policy ports.
- Remove the equal-root shortcut bypass in `world_merge::reduce_root`, enforce `input_schema` in `add_application_handler`, fix creation/deletion classification in `diff_world_roots`, and validate all nodes in `worldcommit::validate_parent_graph` against detached cycles.

## Impact

- **Files**: `src/world_promotion/{current.rs,service.rs,operator/mod.rs,store/outbox.rs,store/transaction.rs}`, `crates/molten-core/src/world_promotion/{operator.rs,planning/mod.rs}`, `src/world_head/parts/service/{p000,p001}/body.rs`, `crates/molten-core/src/world_merge/{admission.rs,planning/application.rs,diff.rs}`, `crates/molten-core/src/worldcommit/restore.rs`, and corresponding companion docs in `docs/technical/`.
- **Verification**: Positive and negative unit/CLI/crash-injection tests covering cross-action grant replay, delimiter injection, mid-dispatch crash recovery, partial promotion read-back, `Stale`/`Uncertain` world-head receipts, unsigned carrier forgery, equal-root excluded/runtime-sensitive merge denial, `input_schema` mismatch, and detached parent-graph cycles.
- **Non-claims**: Fixing local world-state atomicity and signature binding ensures local epistemic honesty; it does not create distributed exactly-once external effect execution.
