## Why

Issue #17 asks for one executable world-promotion profile whose authority, intent closure, and adapter facts come from actual admitted state. The `world-promotion promote` command already verifies signed authority and closure files and rechecks the head under the write transaction, but two gaps remain. The signed closure binds intent references without establishing that the intent material exists or matches the plan, so a missing or mismatched intent could still promote. Retry planning and abandon accept no operator authority (only deny does), and they rebuild the original plan from the caller's `authority_admitted` and `intent_closure_complete` booleans, which the issue requires to be planning inputs only.

## What Changes

- A content-addressed intent-material store under the node storage namespace (`world-promotion/intent-material/<blake3>.preserves`) holds canonical `molten.world-effect-intent-material.v1` records. An intent reference is the BLAKE3 content reference of its material. `molten world-promotion publish-intent` publishes material; publication grants no authority.
- At application time the current-facts adapter reads the material of every plan intent and reports `intent_material_available`; the pure transaction validator denies `IntentMaterialUnavailable` before any head or reservation change when material is missing, non-canonical, readdressed, or differs from the plan (including another adapter).
- `retry-plan` and `abandon` require `--authority` and `--trusted-signer` like `deny`. A pure `validate_operator_authority` decides currentness for all three: admitted, at the reservation generation; retry planning and abandon also bind branch, authority, and policy to the immutable original plan. The original plan is rebuilt independent of the caller's booleans and accepted only when the store holds that exact committed identity. Duplicate-risk and unknown-outcome acknowledgements remain mandatory; no replacement logical identity is created.

## Impact

- **Files**: `crates/molten-core/src/world_promotion/{operator.rs,model/dispatch.rs,model/plan.rs,planning.rs,mod.rs,tests/operator.rs,tests.rs}`; `src/world_promotion/{material.rs,operator.rs,current.rs,service.rs,mod.rs,records/material.rs,records/mod.rs,tests/**}`; `src/cli/runtime/worldpromotion.rs` and `src/cli/runtime/worldpromotion/{material.rs,mutations.rs,retry.rs}`; `tests/worldpromotion{cli,denials}.rs`, `tests/worldpromotionsupport/mod.rs`; `schemas/preserves-boundaries/molten-world-effect-intent-material-v1.preserves`; `docs/world-promotion-current-facts.md`.
- **Testing**: core operator-authority and material-matching decisions; material store round trip, tamper, readdress, and absence; service deny/retry/abandon under stale, revoked, and foreign authority and under replacement requests; the public command matrix (promote, missing/mismatched material, stale/revoked/incomplete denials, replay after a lost result, identity conflict, interrupted attempt reconciliation, retry/abandon/deny authority).
- **Owner / consumer**: the world promotion shell and its operators. Existing request documents must name intent references that address published material; retry-plan and abandon invocations must add an operator grant.
- **Non-claims**: no production dispatcher, no distributed exactly-once effect, no proof that the signer's authority system or policy is correct.
